Delivery of services

Last updated 25 November 2026

We sell software and services. We do not ship physical goods, so nothing is delivered by courier or post.

This page covers Mira Software Solutions and this website only. Mirwe, our product, is a separate service with its own terms and privacy policy on mirwe.com.

Custom software

We deliver custom software in the stages and on the dates set out in the written quote or agreement for that project. Delivery is electronic: we hand over working software, access to it and its source code as the agreement provides.

Support and maintenance

Support is provided remotely during our office hours, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 17:00 South African time, unless your agreement says otherwise.
