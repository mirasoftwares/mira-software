Refunds and cancellations

Last updated 25 November 2026

This page explains how cancellations and refunds work for the custom software, support and maintenance we sell. All amounts are in South African rand.

This page covers Mira Software Solutions and this website only. Mirwe, our product, is a separate service with its own terms and privacy policy on mirwe.com.

Custom software, support and maintenance

This work is done under a written quote or agreement that both sides sign before work starts. That agreement sets out the price, the payment schedule, how either side can cancel and what is refunded if the work stops early.

If you were charged in error

If you believe we charged you by mistake or charged the wrong amount, email info@mirasoftware.co.za with the date and amount. We will look into it and correct any error.

How refunds are paid

When a refund is due, we pay it back to the payment method you used, where that is possible.

Your rights

Nothing on this page limits any right you have under the Consumer Protection Act or the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act.

Contact

Questions about a cancellation or refund: info@mirasoftware.co.za.
