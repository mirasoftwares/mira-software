About Mira Software

We build and run Mirwe, and we build custom software for other businesses. Our engineers have spent more than ten years on payment, loyalty and retail systems.

We are a small, senior team based in Johannesburg. When you contact us, you reach the people who build the product.

What customers pay us for

All prices are in South African rand.

Mirwe subscriptions

Brands pay a monthly subscription to use Mirwe. Plans and prices are published on mirwe.com.

Custom software

We design and build web apps, mobile apps and online stores for businesses, on a quote agreed in writing before work starts.

Support and maintenance

Ongoing care for software we have built, billed monthly on an agreed plan.

Shoppers pay the brand, not us.

When someone buys from a store that runs on Mirwe, the money goes straight to that brand through the brand's own payment provider. Mira Software never holds shoppers' money.

Security and privacy

How we protect brands, shoppers and their information.

Card payments

Handled by licensed, PCI DSS certified payment providers. We never see or store full card numbers.

Encrypted connections

Every page and every sign-in uses an encrypted connection.

POPIA

We collect only what we need. People can ask to see, correct or delete their information.

Stored in South Africa

Mirwe catalogue, stock and order data is stored and processed in South Africa.

Controlled access

Each team member has their own sign-in and sees only what their role allows.

Backups

Data is backed up regularly so it can be restored if something goes wrong.
