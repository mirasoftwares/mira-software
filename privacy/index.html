Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy explains how Mira Software Solutions (Pty) Ltd ("we", "us") handles personal information collected through mirasoftware.co.za, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

This policy covers Mira Software Solutions and this website only. Mirwe, our product, is a separate service. Information about brands and shoppers who use Mirwe is covered by the Mirwe privacy policy on mirwe.com, not by this page.

What we collect

When you send us a message through the contact form, we collect only what you type: your first and last name, your email address, the topic you choose and your message.

This site does not use cookies and does not track you across other sites.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your message and to deal with your enquiry. We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing emails.

Who processes it for us

  • A form delivery provider, which passes your message to our inbox.
  • Our email provider, which stores the message and our reply.
  • Our website host, which serves these pages and, like any web server, sees your IP address and browser type.
  • A web font provider, which serves the typefaces on these pages and sees your IP address and browser type.

Some of these providers may process information outside South Africa, under the safeguards POPIA requires.

How long we keep it

We keep your message only for as long as we need it to deal with your enquiry and any work that follows from it, or for as long as the law requires. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or have it deleted. You can also object to how we use it, or lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Contact

To use any of these rights, or with any question about this policy, contact our Information Officer at info@mirasoftware.co.za.
